=== 7. SUBSIDIARY FINDINGS ===

1. Q6 ChatGPT breakthrough — significance:
   ChatGPT selected thegeolab.net/extractability/ as the SOLE source for
   "What is Extractability in GEO?" and cited it 6 times inline in the
   answer body. This is a different citation pattern from Perplexity (which
   includes tgl in a 7-8 source annotation set). ChatGPT treated the page
   as the single authoritative definition. First appearance after 0/20
   across M1+M2.

2. Q5 Perplexity persistent misread — confirmed 9/9 iterations (M1+M2+M3):
   "How does the GEO Stack work?" resolves to OSGeo/eSEOspace/Elastic/Azure/
   Blender across all 9 Perplexity iterations in all three months.
   Measurement-invalid. Requires query reformulation:
   "How does the GEO Stack work in Generative Engine Optimisation?" or
   "What are the layers of the GEO Stack framework for AI citation?"

3. Q2 Perplexity confirmed null despite Google rank 2:
   0/30 Perplexity across M1+M2+M3 (30 combined iterations checked).
   Google organic rank 2 confirmed in M3 SERP check. AIO async.
   Q2 root cause (corrected 2026-05-29): The 301 (/retrieval-probability-geo-stack/ ->
   /retrieval-probability/) is live, single-hop, and correct to keep; it was already deployed
   (Nginx block dated 2026-04-20), not absent. Crawl lag is NOT the cause: the Q2 null begins at
   M1 (28 March), about three weeks before the old slug or its redirect existed, and persisted
   across M2 and M3 with the redirect in place. The redirect acts on the old slug only; Q2 targets
   the canonical /retrieval-probability/, which serves 200. Most likely cause: query-retrieval
   mismatch on the Q2 phrasing -- the same page is cited 53.3% in E030 (fan-out phrasings) and 0%
   in E014 Q2 ('What is Retrieval Probability in AI search?'). M4 action: do NOT assume exit from
   null; the diagnostic is whether an alternative Q2 phrasing closer to the E030 formulations
   pulls the citation. A query test, not a wait on crawl.

4. Q9 Perplexity query disambiguation confirmed needed:
   Bare "What is System Memory in GEO?" → RAM/GIS/hardware in 6/9 correct
   iterations across M3 (3/3 this month). Reformulation needed:
   "What is System Memory in the GEO Stack framework?"

5. Google AIO all async — SERP-rank scoring applies:
   All 10 queries returned asynchronous_ai_overview=true. No synchronous
   AIO content was captured. Scoring follows E016 precedent: organic rank 1
   = C. Q2 (rank 2) scored X under this rule.
