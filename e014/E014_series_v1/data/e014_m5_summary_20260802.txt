=== 7. SUBSIDIARY FINDINGS ===

1. ChatGPT method change — forced-search (M5 = new baseline):
   M5 ChatGPT leg uses forced-search (browsing explicitly triggered).
   This changes the measurement method from M1-M4 (where ChatGPT could
   choose whether to search). M5 ChatGPT results are NOT directly
   comparable to M1-M4 ChatGPT. M5 establishes the new forced-search
   baseline for future months.

2. Perplexity method change — fresh Chrome incognito profile per query:
   Each query run in a fresh incognito profile to eliminate session bleed
   and personalisation. This tightens measurement validity but means M5
   Perplexity results reflect zero-personalisation conditions.

3. Q9 page substitution — cross-engine pattern:
   All three engines (Perplexity 3/3, ChatGPT 1/1, Google AIO 1/1) cite
   /geo-stack/ when asked about System Memory, NOT /system-memory/.
   The hub page has absorbed the layer page for this query. This is a
   structural pattern, not stochastic: 5/5 = 100% page substitution rate.

4. Q2 breakthrough — from longest null to full saturation:
   /retrieval-probability/ was the longest-running null in E014 (0 citations
   across M1-M4, 120+ checks). M5 = 5/5 across all platforms. This is the
   single largest per-query swing in the study.

5. Q5 entity attribution surfaced on ChatGPT and Google AIO:
   Both ChatGPT and AIO explicitly attribute the GEO Stack to
   'Artur Ferreira at The GEO Lab'. Entity graph work is producing
   visible attribution in AI answers.

6. T2 remains near-null but no longer absolute zero:
   Q10 Perplexity I2 cited /does-geo-work/ — first T2 citation in E014
   history (5 months, 125 T2 checks). Rate: 1/25 = 4%.
   All other T2 queries remain 0%.
